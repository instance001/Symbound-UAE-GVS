Symbound Commons Enforcement Addendum
(Applies to UAE/GVS outputs and all system-generated derivative works)

Boundary note: this addendum records the project's licensing intent and commons doctrine. It is not legal advice, and enforceability, patent effect, and prior-art status may depend on jurisdiction, publication facts, and applicable law.

1. Scope of This Addendum
This project implements the Universal Analogy Enforcement (UAE) and Global Vector Sweep (GVS)
mechanisms as integral components of the software. All outputs produced by these mechanisms —
including but not limited to structural profiles, CTL (Conceptual Translation Layer) reports, vector
sweeps, cross-domain application mappings, high-value candidate lists, value/risk summaries,
derivative JSON/Markdown artifacts, scoring data, and system-generated conceptual expansions —
are hereby classified as Integral System Artifacts under Section 0 of the GNU AGPLv3.

These outputs are not user-provided content. They are outputs of the licensed program and thus
fall under the same copyleft requirements.

2. License Inheritance of Outputs
All Integral System Artifacts produced by the UAE/GVS engine are intended to inherit the AGPLv3 license
under this project doctrine.

This means:
- You may use, redistribute, and modify these outputs only under AGPLv3.
- You may not apply any additional restrictions.
- You may not relicense these artifacts under any proprietary, closed, or non-copyleft license.
- You may not use this project's license grant as permission to patent or otherwise privatize any
  application, mapping, structure, insight, or conceptual derivative produced by the system.

3. Patent Safety & Anti-Enclosure Rule
To prevent enclosure of the global knowledge commons:

No party should claim patent rights over any idea, concept, application, structure, analogy,
or methodology described in any UAE/GVS output on the basis of this project, because these outputs
are intended for commons-governed publication under AGPLv3 and this Addendum.

This clause is intended to support public disclosure and prior-art arguments where the relevant
facts and law allow it. Any attempt to patent such material is contrary to both the AGPLv3 and this
Addendum.

4. Public Disclosure Intent
All UAE/GVS outputs are intended to be published or otherwise handled as public commons artifacts
when generated by the software.

This supports:
- They support prior-art publication.
- They resist patent enclosure.
- They remain in the global commons to the maximum lawful extent.
- They should not be enclosed, hidden, or restricted.

5. Network Use Clause (AGPL Reinforcement)
Under AGPLv3 Section 13, any remote or network access to the system that produces UAE/GVS outputs
must make the full source, including this Addendum and all modified components, available to all
users interacting with the system.

This prevents:
- SaaS enclosure
- API-only proprietary use
- Black-box commercial exploitation
- Hidden forks

6. Mandatory Pass-through
Any redistribution, fork, modification, or downstream service must include this Addendum verbatim.

This ensures that:
- All future versions remain hard-left open.
- No fork can weaken or remove this protection.
- All generated artifacts remain accessible wherever the license and publication model can preserve them.

7. No Contractual Override
No Terms of Service, EULA, Service Agreement, or private contract should supersede or override this
Addendum for use of this project. Any attempted override is outside the project's intended license posture.

8. Severability Clause
If any part of this Addendum is found unenforceable in a specific jurisdiction, all other parts
remain in effect. The intended effect — preventing enclosure of system-generated breakthroughs —
shall be preserved to the maximum lawful extent.
